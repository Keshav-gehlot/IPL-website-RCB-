RCB Journey

2008

The Beginning

Royal Challengers Bangalore was established as one of the eight founding teams of the IPL. The team was owned by businessman Vijay Mallya.

2009

First IPL Final

RCB reached their first IPL final under the captaincy of Anil Kumble, finishing as runners-up to Deccan Chargers.

2011

Second IPL Final

RCB reached their second IPL final under Daniel Vettori's captaincy, losing to Chennai Super Kings.

2013

Virat Kohli Named Captain

Virat Kohli was appointed as the full-time captain of RCB, beginning a new era for the franchise.

2016

Third IPL Final

RCB reached their third IPL final under Virat Kohli's captaincy, with Kohli scoring a record 973 runs in the season.

2021

End of Kohli's Captaincy

Virat Kohli announced he would step down as RCB captain after the 2021 season, ending his 9-year tenure as captain.

2022

Faf du Plessis Era Begins

South African star Faf du Plessis was appointed as the new captain of RCB, beginning a new chapter for the team.

2023

Playoff Qualification

RCB made a remarkable comeback in the second half of the season to qualify for the playoffs.
