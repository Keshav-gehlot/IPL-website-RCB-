Frequently Asked Questions

How can I purchase tickets for RCB matches?

Tickets for RCB home matches can be purchased through our official website or authorized ticketing partners. RCB Fan Club members get early access to ticket sales. For group bookings or corporate boxes, please contact our ticket support team.

How do I join the RCB Fan Club?

You can join the RCB Fan Club by visiting the Fan Zone section of our website and selecting a membership tier that suits you. Membership benefits include exclusive merchandise, early access to tickets, and special events with players.

Where can I purchase official RCB merchandise?

Official RCB merchandise is available through our online store, at the M. Chinnaswamy Stadium during match days, and at select retail partners across India. All authentic merchandise features official RCB hologram tags.

How can I apply for trials or selection camps for RCB?

RCB conducts talent scouting and selection camps periodically. Information about upcoming trials is announced on our official website and social media channels. For professional cricketers, please have your cricket association or agent contact our talent acquisition team.

How can I request player appearances or interviews?

For player appearances, interviews, or media requests, please contact our media relations team with details of your request. Please note that player availability is subject to team schedules and commitments, especially during the IPL season.
